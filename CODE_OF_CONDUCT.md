# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, welcoming place for everyone who uses or contributes to it, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback graciously, especially in code review.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful to the people reading it later.
- Respect other people's time and privacy. Never share someone else's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing or spam.
- Publishing private information (including credentials, tokens or customer data) without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report a problem privately through the repository's **Security tab, "Report a vulnerability"**, or by contacting a maintainer on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
